Title: HS Classification and Tariff Determination

The Harmonized System (HS) is a standardized numerical method of classifying traded products.  
Each item imported or exported must be assigned an HS code, usually six to ten digits.  
The first six digits are international, while the last few are country-specific extensions.

Key steps:
1. Identify the product’s material composition and use.
2. Match the product with the most specific heading and subheading in the tariff schedule.
3. Verify with customs rulings or explanatory notes.

Incorrect classification can cause:
- Underpayment or overpayment of duties.
- Customs delays or penalties.
- Loss of preferential treatment under free trade agreements.
