Title: Rules of Origin and Preferential Tariffs

Rules of Origin determine the economic nationality of goods.  
They decide whether products qualify for lower or zero tariffs under trade agreements.

Two main types:
1. Preferential: Used in Free Trade Agreements (e.g., ASEAN, EU).
2. Non-preferential: Used for general trade policy and statistics.

Criteria for origin:
- Wholly obtained goods (e.g., crops, minerals).
- Substantial transformation (value-added or change in tariff classification).
- Regional Value Content (RVC) percentage threshold.

To claim preferential rates:
- Obtain a Certificate of Origin (e.g., Form D for ASEAN).
- Keep supporting records for at least five years.
