Title: Tariff Compliance Documentation Requirements

Proper documentation supports accurate customs clearance.  
Essential documents include:
- Commercial Invoice: lists value, currency, and terms.
- Packing List: identifies package contents and weights.
- Bill of Lading or Air Waybill: transport contract proof.
- Certificate of Origin: proof for preferential tariff eligibility.
- Import/Export Declaration: formal submission to customs.

Common issues:
- Inconsistent data across documents.
- Missing HS codes or incorrect values.
- Expired certificates or unsigned forms.

Companies should maintain digital archives for audit readiness and compliance tracking.
