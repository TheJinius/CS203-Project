Pikachu
Species: Mouse Pokémon
Type: Electric
Height: 0.4 m
Weight: 6.0 kg
Abilities: Static (Hidden Ability: Lightning Rod)
Evolution: Evolves from Pichu with high friendship, and into Raichu when exposed to a Thunder Stone
Description: Pikachu stores electricity in its cheeks and releases it when threatened. It is intelligent, loyal, and often lives near humans or forests.
