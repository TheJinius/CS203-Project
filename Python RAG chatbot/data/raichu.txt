Raichu
Species: Mouse Pokémon
Type: Electric
Height: 0.8 m
Weight: 30.0 kg
Abilities: Static (Hidden Ability: Lightning Rod)
Evolution: Evolves from Pikachu when exposed to a Thunder Stone (can become Alolan Raichu in certain regions)
Description: Raichu’s body stores strong electrical energy. It can glow in the dark from electric charge and can deliver high-voltage shocks.
